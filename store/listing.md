## en-US

**Display name**: Alya — AI Translator
**Short description**: Instant AI translation for anything you select, type, copy or capture.
**Description**:
Alya puts AI translation one keystroke away, everywhere on Windows.

- Select any text and press Alt+Q — get an instant translation
- Type into a floating panel (Alt+E) and translate as you write
- Copy text anywhere and let the clipboard monitor translate it automatically
- Capture any region of the screen (Alt+S / Alt+D) for OCR and translation

Bring your own key: Alya works with any OpenAI-compatible API (DeepSeek by default),
so you stay in control of model, cost and privacy. Your API key is encrypted locally;
text recognition for screenshots runs on your device.

Features: mutual & directed translation scenes · streaming output · auto-translate on
typing pause (configurable delay) · retranslate when you edit · OCR with editable,
auto-cleaned results (line-break and dash normalization) · on-screen capture hint ·
connection test from Settings · one-click restore to defaults · custom hotkeys ·
dark & light themes · 9 interface languages.

Privacy: no telemetry, no accounts, no ads. OCR runs locally.
